A practical reading of the page

The difference is not more decoration.

Before · AI-looking signal

Everything is polished. Nothing is situated.

Uniform cards, default hierarchy, and interchangeable emphasis can make a page feel assembled rather than authored.

Read for: repetition, weightless claims, and visual certainty without a point of view.
After · editorial signal

Structure carries an argument.

Fewer elements, deliberate pacing, and visible source boundaries let the reader understand what the page is choosing to say.

Read for: hierarchy, restraint, and a clear relationship between evidence and interpretation.
